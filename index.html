About Us

From India's Origins
to the World.

At Exsealoop and KK Global Trade, we bring the richness of India's agricultural heritage to customers around the world. As a trusted exporter of premium spices, pulses, millets, superfoods and natural food ingredients, we partner with carefully selected farmers and certified processing facilities to deliver products that meet international quality standards.

Driven by a passion for excellence, we combine traditional sourcing expertise with modern processing, quality assurance and efficient global logistics — every product selected, processed and packed with a focus on purity, consistency, food safety and customer satisfaction.

  1. Sourcing

    Farms of Andhra Pradesh

    Guntur chillies, established turmeric belts and graded cardamom, sourced close to our Kurnool and Visakhapatnam bases.

  2. Processing

    Sortex-Cleaned, Lab-Tested

    Modern cleaning, grading and sorting, with COA and MRL testing available on request for every shipment.

  3. Delivery

    From Farm to Your Racks

    Reliable FOB logistics connecting our partner farms to importers, retailers and manufacturers in 30+ countries.

Our Mission

To deliver authentic, high-quality Indian agricultural products to global markets while supporting sustainable sourcing, maintaining uncompromising quality, and creating lasting value for our customers and supply partners.

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized Indian export brand known for reliability, premium quality, ethical business practices, and customer-focused service.
